# TargetForTime		
_Quick! We are ruuning out of time!_	
Shoot the targets and keep the countdown alive! Once it hits 0, its game over!

# Instructions		
Start with 25s. Each target hit may add upto 0.5s depending on distance, but you don't have time to worry about this!	
Each miss deducts 1s from your countdown, so try to hit the targets! _It is called "Target For Time" for a reason_	
After 30s of gameplay, **SHOWDOWN** occurs! Each target will give half the time, and 30s later, your countdown will decrease **twice** as fast!	
_This is to speed up gameplay and reduce strain on your hand_
